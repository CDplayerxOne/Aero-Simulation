Model notes

Airflow and pressure

A three-dimensional potential-flow solve diverts air around a voxel representation of the car above a slip ground plane. Traces follow the steady velocity field with approximate wheel wakes and wing-tip vortices. Amber tracers sample a prescribed counter-rotating vortex pair inside the two floor tunnels. The reduced-order tunnel model follows the local passage height through the throat and diffuser. Vortex strength is illustrative. Moving highlights show relative travel time in slow motion.

The surface map shows estimated pressure coefficient, Cₚ = (p − p∞)/q. Near-surface speed, surface orientation and prescribed wing and floor loading shape the estimate. The colour scale is fixed across speeds.

Loads and scale

Re = ρUL/μ, with L = 5.50 m and μ = 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s. Loads use q = ½ρU² and reference area A = 1.50 m². The illustrative whole-car lift and drag curves depend on rear wing incidence and yaw. They are independent of the surface pressure colours.

Model limits

This generic ground-effect chassis has no team-specific calibration. Boundary layers, wheel rotation effects, transient turbulence and stall are unresolved. Use the results to compare settings qualitatively.
